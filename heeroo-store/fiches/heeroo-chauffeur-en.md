# Heeroo Driver (chauffeur) — fiche magasin en anglais

Langue à ajouter : **English (U.S.)** sur les deux magasins. Les champs ci-dessous
sont à coller tels quels ; les limites de caractères sont celles des magasins et
chaque bloc les respecte.

---

## App Store Connect

**App Name** (30 max)

```
Heeroo Driver
```

**Subtitle** (30 max)

```
Drive, earn, paid on Monday
```

**Promotional Text** (170 max — modifiable sans nouvelle soumission)

```
Ride requests from passengers nearby, your earnings updated after every trip, and a payout every Monday morning.
```

**Keywords** (100 max, séparés par des virgules, sans espace après la virgule)

```
driver,taxi driver,chauffeur,vtc,rideshare,earnings,ride requests,private hire,wave
```

**Description** (4000 max)

```
Heeroo Driver is the app for drivers working with Heeroo. Go online, receive ride requests from passengers near you, and get paid every week.

RIDE REQUESTS NEAR YOU
When a passenger books nearby, the request reaches you with the pickup address, the destination, the distance and what the trip pays. Accept it or let it pass — it is your call.

YOUR EARNINGS, ALWAYS UP TO DATE
Every completed trip updates your balance straight away. Cash trips, card trips and Wave trips are all accounted for in the same place, with the commission shown on each one.

PAID EVERY MONDAY
Your available balance is paid out every Monday morning: by bank transfer in Europe, by Wave in West Africa. You can also see what is still on hold and when it will be released.

SIGN UP FROM THE APP
Register, upload your licence, your insurance and your vehicle documents, and follow your approval status without leaving the app.

BUILT FOR THE ROAD
Navigation hands off to the map app you already use. Your position is shared with your passenger only while a trip is running, so they can see you coming.
```
