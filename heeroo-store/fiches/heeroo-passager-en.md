**Description** (4000 max)

```
Heeroo is a private-hire ride service. Open the app, set your pickup point and your destination, see the fare before you confirm, and a driver nearby comes to you.

KNOW THE PRICE BEFORE YOU BOOK
Pick your vehicle category and Heeroo shows the estimated fare up front. No meter running, no surprise at the end of the trip.

FOLLOW YOUR DRIVER ON THE MAP
As soon as a driver accepts, you see them approaching in real time, with their name, photo, vehicle and plate number. Message them in the app if you need to, without sharing your phone number.

PAY THE WAY YOU LIKE
- Cash, directly to the driver
- Card, charged automatically at the end of the trip
- Wave, in Senegal and across West Africa

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
Your bookings, your receipts and your notifications stay in the app. Rate your driver at the end of each trip, and reach support from your profile.

WHERE HEEROO WORKS
Heeroo operates across Europe and West Africa, with local fares and local payment methods in each country.
```
